Solar Quotation OS — Business Operating System Workflow

Version 1.0 — September 2026

1. Executive Summary

The Solar Quotation OS is a cloud-based, centralized platform that manages the complete solar sales cycle — from capturing a lead to generating a professionally branded quotation and following up until the deal is won.

One action updates everything automatically. When a quotation is generated, the system sizes the array from the customer's monthly consumption, selects panels/inverters/batteries, computes equipment + services + margin + tax, calculates monthly savings and payback, marks the lead as Quoted, logs the activity, and makes a branded PDF ready to send — all instantly.

The system is accessible from any device with a browser — desktop, tablet, or phone — and all users share the same live data.
